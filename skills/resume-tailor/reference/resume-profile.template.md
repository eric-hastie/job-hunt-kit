# Resume profile

Copy this file to `~/job-hunt/resume-profile.md` and fill in every field. The
resume-tailor skill reads it at the start of every run and stops if a field is
blank.

## Files

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| MASTER_PDF (local PDF export of the master resume; the source of every FIND string) | `/full/path/to/My Resume (Generic).pdf` |
| DOC_URL (the master resume in Google Docs; copies are made from it, it is never edited) | `https://docs.google.com/document/d/.../edit` |
| OUTPUT_ROOT (one subfolder per company is created here) | `/full/path/to/Job Hunt` |
| FILENAME (pattern for the Doc copy and the PDF) | `First Last Resume <Month Year> (<Company>)` |

## Who I am

Two to five lines, only what bears on wording: current title, years of
experience, the domains and products you have worked in, who you sell to or work
with, and the vocabulary you use for your methods.

## My own hard rules

Anything that must never appear or change. Examples: "never mention the side
project", "never reference the gap in 2024", "keep the Acme bullet word for word".
